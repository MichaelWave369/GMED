03 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Convergence is not confirmation.

When two methods agree, they establish numerical consistency for a chosen mathematical model. To test whether the model describes nature, we still need independently verified force implementations, reliable historical source constraints and a gravity-aware orbit determination.

Close-approach exclusions and step budgets make the program refuse scenarios it cannot handle safely.
