04 / RESEARCH DISCIPLINE

What you are actually looking at.

The 11 trajectories are alternative orbital solutions published by Matney, each shown on its own proposed June date. They were selected from a larger search and are not independent observations or equally likely samples.

Earth is reconstructed with NASA JPL’s long-range approximate formula, and the comet follows an ideal parabolic, Sun-only orbit. The ancient time conversion and precession are approximate. An extremely close Earth encounter requires a higher-fidelity model for scientific reproduction.
